ORIGINAL STATIC / SKETCH STORYBOARD

He Took Everything

Format
Vertical short drama (9:16)
Genre
Crime / Revenge / Family Drama

Concept

A successful businessman's wife disappears after an attack. As he follows the clues through fire, rain and a city-wide search, all evidence points to her father.

Открыть исходную статику ↗
FRAME 01

The Perfect Life

Visual / Action

A successful businessman stands beside his wife overlooking the city. A brief glimpse of their happy family.

Audio / Dialogue

Male VO: “I had everything - my business, my wife, my children.”

FRAME 02

The Enemy

Visual / Action

At a family dinner, his wife's father watches him coldly. The businessman notices the hostility.

Audio / Dialogue

Male VO: “But her father always wanted me gone.”

FRAME 03

The Call

Visual / Action

Outside his office late at night, the businessman answers his phone in the pouring rain. His expression changes instantly.

Audio / Dialogue

Phone voice: “Your wife is missing. Her car is on fire.”
SFX: Driving rain, distant sirens and helicopter blades.

FRAME 04

The Discovery

Visual / Action

His wife’s empty car burns in the rain. Police lights flash across the wet street while a helicopter searches overhead. Among the chaos, the businessman finds his father-in-law’s distinctive cufflink beside an envelope.

Audio / Dialogue

Male VO: “It wasn’t just an accident.”
SFX: Crackling fire, heavy rain, police radio and a helicopter search overhead.

FRAME 05

The Confrontation

Visual / Action

Still soaked from the rain, the businessman enters his father-in-law’s dark office and throws the evidence onto his desk. The older man slowly smiles.

Audio / Dialogue

Businessman: “It was you.”
Father-in-law: “And you still don’t know why.”

FRAME 06

Cliffhanger / CTA

Visual / Action

Close-up of the businessman as the father-in-law reveals a photograph. Cut to black before the audience sees it.

Audio / Dialogue

SFX: Sharp cinematic hit.
On-screen text: “Create your own AI series.”
CTA: “Create Now.”

Creative direction

Calm family setup → sudden disappearance → fire, rain and search chaos → evidence of betrayal → confrontation → unresolved twist. Orange flames, red-and-blue police lights, rain on wet asphalt and a helicopter search beam echo the original static. The story remains unfinished to create curiosity, while the final CTA connects the cinematic sequence to AI-series creation.
